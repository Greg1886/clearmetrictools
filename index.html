How this calculator works

What it compares

Two ways to invest the same money for the same number of years:

  • Option A: a currency conversion (FX) fee on every deposit, a fixed order fee on every monthly purchase, and the fund's yearly fee (TER).
  • Option B: a low-cost setup with its own FX fee and TER, and no order fee.

The red figure, "wealth lost to fees", is how much less Option A leaves you at the end.

How the numbers are calculated

  • Your first deposit pays the FX fee once.
  • Each month, the FX fee (and for Option A the order fee) comes off your deposit before it is invested.
  • The balance then grows by the gross return divided by 12, and one twelfth of the yearly TER is taken from it.
  • "Direct fees paid" adds up every FX fee, order fee and TER charge. "Wealth lost to fees" is the gap between the two final values. It is larger than the difference in fees, because money paid in fees also stops growing.

Why small fees matter

With €500 a month, a 0.25% FX fee and a €2 order fee cost €3.25 a month: 0.65% of every deposit, gone before it earns anything. A TER of 0.38% on a €100,000 portfolio costs €380 a year, against €150 at 0.15%. Both repeat every year, and the gap compounds.

Worked example

Start with €10,000, add €500 a month for 20 years, with a 9.5% gross yearly return:

  • Option A (0.25% FX, €2 per order, 0.38% TER) ends at about €400,655, after €12,209 in direct fees.
  • Option B (0.02% FX, 0.15% TER, no order fee) ends at about €416,166, after €4,654 in direct fees.

Option A leaves you about €15,512 less, although the direct fees differ by only about €7,555.

About the quick scenarios

The scenario buttons fill in example fee levels for common comparisons. They are illustrations, not current price lists. Brokers change their fees, so check your broker's current fee schedule, and the fund's Key Information Document (KID) for its ongoing charges.

What it leaves out

  • Taxes, inflation and changes in exchange rates.
  • It applies the FX fee to every deposit, as when you buy a fund priced in another currency. If your fund trades in your own currency, set the FX fee to 0.
  • Account or custody fees, percentage-based order fees, bid-ask spreads and fees when you sell.
